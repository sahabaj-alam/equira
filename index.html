How these numbers are calculated

Monthly payment (principal + interest): M = P × i / (1 − (1 + i)^-n), where P = loan balance, i = annual rate ÷ 12, and n = number of monthly payments. (A 0% rate simplifies to P / n.)

Refinance break-even: monthly saving = current payment − new payment; break-even = closing costs / monthly saving, rounded up to whole months. The lifetime figure compares total scheduled payments — the current loan's remaining payments vs. the new loan plus closing costs — held to term and not discounted for the time value of money.

PMI drop-off: your balance is amortized forward month by month. Under the U.S. Homeowners Protection Act, you can request cancellation at 80% loan-to-value and it auto-terminates at 78%, measured against the original property value and the loan's amortization schedule.

All figures are principal + interest only (no taxes, insurance, or HOA) and are estimates — verify with your lender before acting.
